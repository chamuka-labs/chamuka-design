# Layout and grid

## Structure from hairlines, not shadows

- Every box, card, panel and image frame gets the same 1px border on all four sides: `--db-border`, `--db-border-strong` or `--db-border-hover`.
- **No side or accent borders.** No thick left bars, no coloured top strips.
- To emphasise one item, turn its whole 1px edge ember. Nothing else changes.
- **No shadows of any kind.** Depth comes from fill steps: bg → surface → raised.
- Radii: 2px for buttons, inputs and chips; 4px for panels, cards and dialogs. Nothing rounder except status dots.

## Page frame

- Container 1200px (`max-w-daybreak` / `--db-container`), gutter 20–32px, 12 columns. Text measure 60–68ch.
- Section gap `clamp(96px, 8vw + 48px, 192px)` (`--db-section`).
- The section pattern is the **ledger**: a 5/7 split, mono number and label on the left, a framed panel with the artefact on the right. It stacks on mobile, label first.
- Footer is quiet: the mark, the licence line and links on the grid, a 1px ember rule above. No big wordmark, no marquee.

## Stacking

Use `Stack` for vertical rhythm on the 4px base: 16px between related items, 24px between groups, 32–48px between blocks.
