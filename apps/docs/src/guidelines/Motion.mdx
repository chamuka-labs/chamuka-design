# Motion

Durations: 120ms fast, 220ms base, 480ms slow. Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.1, 1)` (`--db-ease`).

Animate colour, edge and at most a 4px translate. Nothing bounces, nothing loops and nothing moves on its own on load. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` zeroes the durations.

Toasts may linger 4–6s; they must be dismissible and must not steal focus from a form control the person is using.
